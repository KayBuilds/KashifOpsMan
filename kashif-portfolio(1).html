About Me

The person behind

I'm a Senior Supplier Operations Manager at Elsevier (RELX India Pvt Ltd), Chennai — with 17+ years building and governing content pipelines for the world's largest scholarly databases.

My work spans supplier governance across 6 global vendors (Straive, Innodata, Lumina Datamatics, Aptara-AiP, EPAM and others), content processing workflows for Reaxys, Scopus, Embase & RTB product lines, and increasingly, AI-augmented automation of operations that used to take entire teams.

Outside Elsevier, I run The Floor Plan — a commercial property consultancy across Chennai and Bangalore — and I'm actively building AI-native SaaS products in my own time.

I'm driven by one question: how do we make complex operations invisible?
